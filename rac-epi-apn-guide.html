RAC • EPI • APN — How It Works

DOME uses RAC as the common risk foundation. Standard DIMS execution records use EPI for relative execution priority; genuine safety hazards may use the Air Force-style APN method instead.

FOUNDATION

RAC — Risk Assessment Code

RAC is produced by the intersection of Severity and Probability. Lower RAC numbers represent higher primary risk.

DIMS EXECUTION

EPI — Execution Priority Index

EPI breaks ties and guides execution sequencing for normal DIMS projects, tasks, defects, and corrective actions.

SAFETY HAZARD

APN — Abatement Priority Number

APN is used when a record is classified as a genuine safety hazard and the safety abatement methodology applies.

1. RAC — Risk First

Severity×Probability→RAC 1–5→EPI or APN

RAC remains the primary risk classification. EPI/APN do not replace RAC; they provide the next level of priority discrimination.

2. APN — Safety-Hazard Priority

DOME preserves the adopted Air Force method:

APN = RAC (CEI)    |    CEI = C ÷ (M × E)

C = abatement cost; M = Severity/Probability multiplier; E = average personnel exposed daily.

Priority rule: compare CEI values only among records with the same RAC. Within the same RAC, the lower CEI receives the higher relative abatement priority.

3. EPI — DIMS Execution Priority

EPI uses the same visual logic as APN, but its governed factors reflect DIMS work rather than physical-hazard abatement.

ImpactHow broadly and seriously the unresolved issue affects DIMS/DOME operations, dependencies, mission, users, or deliverables.
AIT — AI TimeEstimated AI work required to diagnose, build, test, correct, document, and verify the action.
HIT — Human Interaction TimeEstimated human time required for review, decisions, manual actions, confirmations, credentials, testing, and back-and-forth interaction.
ERE = AIT + HIT
Stage 1 governance: EPI currently discloses its governed factors and estimated resolution effort. The final numerical EPI formula/matrix remains under validation and will not be presented as institutional doctrine until approved.
