HOW THE THLIPSIS SĒMEION INDEX IS CALCULATED

TSI is not a conventional danger or risk score. It measures observable development and convergence of biblically defined signs, compares current conditions with historical measurement baselines, and supports a separate estimate of prophetic proximity without setting dates.

APOSTOLIC PROPHETIC REFERENCE ERA — APPROXIMATELY AD 30–70

1. What is the reference point?

Biblical Reference Standard: Scripture defines the signs, prophetic conditions and watch boundaries.

Apostolic Prophetic Reference Era: approximately AD 30–70 marks the first-century period in which Jesus and the apostles gave the prophetic instructions SHAMAR uses.

Historical Measurement Baseline: each measurable domain uses the most defensible historical data period for determining what has normally occurred. There is no single statistical baseline forced across all domains.

BIBLICAL STANDARDWhat Scripture says to watch.
AD 30–70 REFERENCE ERAThe world in which those warnings were given.
HISTORICAL BASELINEWhat has normally occurred in measured data.
CURRENT CONDITIONWhat validated evidence shows now.

2. The six weighted TSI factors

TSI = Biblical Specificity 25% + Baseline Departure 20% + Structural Readiness 20% + Acceleration / Trend 15% + Global Reach 10% + Cross-Domain Convergence 10%
25%Biblical Specificity

How directly the measured condition corresponds to the defined biblical sign or prophetic condition.

20%Baseline Departure

How far the current measurement has moved from the established historical norm.

20%Structural Readiness

Whether institutions, systems, capabilities, alliances or mechanisms actually exist and are materially developed.

15%Acceleration / Trend

Whether the measured development is increasing, intensifying, spreading or becoming more persistent.

10%Global Reach

How geographically and institutionally widespread the measured condition is.

10%Cross-Domain Convergence

Whether independent prophetic domains are developing together rather than one isolated sign driving the conclusion.

3. Biblical Specificity Cap

A domain cannot receive an extremely high TSI score merely because a development is global, rapid or technologically mature. Biblical Specificity places a ceiling on the final score:

Specificity 0 → maximum 19   •   1 → 39   •   2 → 59   •   3 → 79   •   4 → 100.

This protects TSI from treating an impressive modern development as prophetic fulfillment when its relationship to the biblical condition is indirect or incomplete.

These are convergence stages, not threat levels and not conventional risk severity.

5. Prophetic Proximity — a separate judgment

The TSI number measures convergence. Prophetic Proximity answers a different question: based on the combined observable signs, how near do present conditions appear to the prophetic conditions Jesus and the apostles described?

DISTANTMost defined prophetic conditions remain close to historical background.
APPROACHINGMultiple signs are observable, but major structures and convergence remain incomplete.
NEARSeveral major prophetic conditions are materially developed and increasingly converging.
VERY NEARBroad sustained convergence is present across multiple high-specificity biblical indicators.
AT THE DOORSExceptional multi-domain convergence with the defined scriptural conditions; Matthew 24:33 language, never a date prediction.

Important: Prophetic Proximity is not a direct conversion such as “TSI 80 = At the Doors.” A high score in one domain cannot produce that conclusion by itself. The assessment requires breadth across independent signs, evidence completeness, cross-domain convergence, significant missing conditions and counter-evidence.

6. Assessment Confidence

HIGH

Strong, current, independently supported and well-documented evidence.

MODERATE

Adequate evidence with meaningful limitations, incomplete coverage or a narrower evidence base.

LOW

Preliminary, limited, conflicting, stale or materially incomplete evidence.

Confidence is not the TSI score and does not mean danger level. It describes how well-supported the assessment is.

7. Scriptural boundary

SHAMAR does not calculate when the Tribulation will begin.
It assesses observable prophetic proximity based upon the signs Scripture tells believers to watch. The system may estimate apparent nearness; it must not declare the appointed date, day, hour, exact remaining duration or prophetic certainty unsupported by Scripture and evidence.
