GRĒGOREŌ™

Greek:
γρηγορέω · grēgoreō · “watch / stay awake / be vigilant”
Why This Name:
GRĒGOREŌ was chosen because this module watches conditions, patterns, evidence, and developments that require sustained vigilance. Its purpose is observation and discernment—not system protection or infrastructure security.
Mark 13:37 (KJV):
“And what I say unto you I say unto all, Watch.”
Function:
Provides governed Peace & Safety intelligence by monitoring relevant domains, organizing evidence, assessing convergence, and presenting conditions that require attention or further review.
