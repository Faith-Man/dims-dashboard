GEGRAPTAI™

Greek:
γέγραπται · gegraptai · “It is written”
Why This Name:
GEGRAPTAI was chosen because Scripture is the written authority against which teaching, intelligence, direction, and confession are tested and grounded. Jesus repeatedly answered temptation and error with the governing declaration, “It is written.”
Matthew 4:4 (KJV):
“But he answered and said, It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God.”
Function:
Provides a focused daily Kingdom briefing for Scripture-grounded intelligence, directives, and confession without exposing unnecessary system machinery.
