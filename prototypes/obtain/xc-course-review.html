1 · VISION

Establish the destination. Define measurable MARKs and use IMAGINATION to form and rehearse the internal picture.

Includes: MARK · IMAGINATION · Visualization

2 · LOGOS

Establish the governing truth, foundational principle and standard for the journey.

Includes: Daily Word · Foundation

3 · RHEMA

Apply truth to the present situation: today's direction, coaching application and emphasis.

Includes: Today’s Application

4 · FAITH

Believe and act upon established truth, supported by progress, training history and evidence.

Includes: Belief · Expectation

5 · DECLARATION

Bring words into agreement with truth, belief and committed action.

Includes: My Declaration

6 · DOiT™

Execute the work. TRAIN prepares. FIGHT contends when challenged. PRESS continues toward the MARK.

Includes: TRAIN · FIGHT · PRESS

7 · RESULT

Record what execution actually produced. Results, PRs and milestones provide evidence. PRIZE lives here as the objective/reward.

Includes: PRIZE · PRs · Results

8 · i3E™ REVIEW

Review the RESULT through Integrity governance, Effectiveness, Efficiency and Excellence. Exact review mechanics remain subject to final approval.

Review: Integrity · Effectiveness · Efficiency · Excellence

9 · IMPROVE

Turn evidence and review into correction, adjustment, coach feedback and the next action or MARK.

Includes: Feedback · Next MARK
