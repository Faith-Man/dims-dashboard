OIKONOMOS™

Greek:
οἰκονόμος · oikonomos · “steward / household manager”
Why This Name:
OIKONOMOS was chosen because stewardship is the faithful administration of what another has entrusted. The module therefore governs responsibility, condition, obligations, resources, and accountable care rather than ownership for its own sake.
Luke 12:42 (KJV):
“And the Lord said, Who then is that faithful and wise steward, whom his lord shall make ruler over his household, to give them their portion of meat in due season?”
Function:
Stewardship and administration of resources, obligations, records, assets, financial matters, property, renewals, estate, and other entrusted responsibilities. OIKONOMOS owns the stewardship state; TETELESTAI™ owns the action required concerning it.
Stewardship
Attention Now

Due dates, renewals, exceptions, thresholds, balances, and other stewardship conditions belong here when governed integrations are available. Actionable conditions should flow to TETELESTAI rather than require duplicate manual entry.

Finance & Cash Flow

Financial stewardship, cash flow, budgets, goals, accounts, liabilities, and governed financial condition. This is a major internal workspace within OIKONOMOS, not the whole module.

Assets, Property & Vehicles

Property, vehicles, equipment, and other entrusted assets with continuing stewardship state and history.

Insurance, Taxes & Official Records

Coverage, tax matters, official records, filing status, documentation, and related administrative obligations.

Contracts, Subscriptions & Renewals

Contracts, recurring commitments, subscriptions, renewals, expiration dates, and obligations requiring continuing oversight.

Estate, Beneficiary & Legacy

Estate, beneficiary, inheritance, legacy, and other long-term stewardship records and responsibilities.

Stewardship History

Material changes, decisions, conditions, and stewardship history across the domain. Work required to act on those records flows to TETELESTAI.

Historical financial lineage: The governing architecture records that OIKONOMOS evolved from the earlier Daniel’s Covenant Blessing Center / Kingdom Financial Command Center concept. The original emphasis was financial, while the present governed domain is intentionally broader than finance alone.
Integration boundary: Financial-institution connectivity must use a specialized financial-data integration. Banking credentials and financial access secrets must never be committed to GitHub or exposed in client code.
