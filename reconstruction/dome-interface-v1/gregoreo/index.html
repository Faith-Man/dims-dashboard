GRĒGOREŌ was chosen because this module watches conditions, patterns, evidence, and developments that require sustained vigilance. Its purpose is watchfulness and discernment—not system guarding or infrastructure protection.

“And what I say unto you I say unto all, Watch.”

Provides governed Peace & Safety intelligence by monitoring relevant domains, organizing evidence, assessing convergence, and presenting conditions that require attention or further review.
