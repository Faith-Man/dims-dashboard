## Governing methodology

**IAM™ — IDENTIFY • ASSESS • MITIGATE** is the current governing methodology of the Risk Assessment Dome (RAD™).

The earlier RAD™ circular-matrix wording **ASSESS RISK → DETERMINE PRIORITY → GOVERN EXECUTION** is retained only as historical development context and has been superseded by IAM™.

## Development lineage

Air Force Safety / ORM Training → DIMS Projects & Tasks Prioritization → Probability × Severity Risk Matrix → RAD™ Development and Refinement → IAM™ — Identify • Assess • Mitigate → MED™ Marriage Application → Broader Life Application → IAM / “I AM” Discovery → Exodus 3 and the theological teaching.

IAM™ did not develop overnight. It emerged through investigation, discovery, application, evaluation, and refinement while solving actual project/task prioritization and risk-management problems. The discovery that multiple issues can share the same risk code while requiring different execution priority was one catalyst for further refinement.

## Application

**IDENTIFY** — Clearly identify the issue, problem, hazard, barrier, requirement, intended condition, identity, design, or purpose.

**ASSESS** — Evaluate the present condition against the intended condition, including risk, probability, severity, consequences, barriers, relationships, and other factors relevant to significance and priority.

**MITIGATE** — Determine and execute appropriate actions to reduce, control, correct, overcome, strengthen, restore, or otherwise address the identified problem or barrier. Mitigation does not always mean elimination.

IAM™ extends beyond RAD™. MED™ demonstrated its application to marriage by identifying God’s design for marriage, assessing present conditions and barriers against that design, and mitigating departures or problems. The framework then broadened to life, ministry, relationships, finances, projects, decisions, personal development, calling, and purpose.
