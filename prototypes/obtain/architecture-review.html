GOVERNING STRUCTURE — REVIEW BEFORE ACTIVATION

Spartans1st™ Platform Architecture

Current architecture review for the separate Spartans1st cross-country application. THE XC COURSE™ is the athlete-development operating framework; it is not the entire application.

Review status only. This updates the architecture-review page on the isolated prototype branch. It does not replace the functioning splash/home experience and does not modify production DIMS/DOME.
LAYER 1 — FOUNDATION & DYNAMIC CONTENT

Foundation + Content Engine

Spartan StandardDynamic LOGOSDynamic RHEMADynamic DECLARATION

The content engine supplies changing material to XC COURSE stages; it is not itself another stage. Scripture-based content remains voluntary/optional in a public-school context while core coaching and training functions remain available independently.

LAYER 2 — THE XC COURSE™

Nine Primary Stages

VISION → LOGOS → RHEMA → FAITH → DECLARATION → DOiT™ → RESULT → i3E™ REVIEW → IMPROVE → VISION

VISION
Destination.

MARK = measurable target.
IMAGINATION = internal picture/rehearsal.
Visualization = practical technique within Imagination.
DOiT™
Execution.

TRAIN = disciplined preparation.
FIGHT = contend through challenge.
PRESS = continue pursuing the MARK.
RESULT
Evidence of what execution produced.

PRIZE = reward/objective pursued or attained.
Results and PRs feed review.

WIN™ remains the center/outcome, never an additional stage. IMPROVE returns the athlete to a better-informed VISION.

Longitudinal Development Record

This layer records evidence of development over time rather than competing with the XC COURSE hierarchy.

CROSS-PLATFORM GOVERNANCE

IAM™ → RAD → RAC → Mitigation / Action

IAM: IdentifyIAM: AssessIAM: MitigateRAD: Probability × SeverityRAC: Approved Risk Communication

IAM can operate anywhere on the Course. RAD is used when structured assessment is warranted. RAC communicates the governed assessment. No RAC categories, colors or thresholds are invented on this page.

LAYER 4 — TEAM & COACH OPERATIONS

Team Management

Team RosterAthlete ProfilesAttendanceTraining AssignmentsTraining CompletionMeets / EventsCoach AnnouncementsIAM Follow-UpTeam i3E

Authorized coaches see the athlete-development information needed to guide training, review readiness and performance indicators, document follow-up and adjust training within their role and authority.

LAYER 5 — PROGRAM ADMINISTRATION

Resources & Stewardship

BudgetEquipment / NeedsResourcesForms / DocumentsSchedulingEventsSeasons / Permissions

Administrative capabilities support the program without cluttering the athlete's daily execution experience.

ROLE-BASED EXPERIENCE

Athlete • Coach • Parent

Athlete — own experienceCoach — authorized development accessParent — linked-athlete parent-visible progress

Parent-visible means visibility, not parent approval. Access is role-based and authorized, with the athlete surface kept focused while coach and administrative functions expose the additional tools required by those roles.
