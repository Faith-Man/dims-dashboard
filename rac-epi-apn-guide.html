Definitions

RAD™ — Risk Assessment Dome is the DIMS cross-system risk-assessment service and workspace. It supports governed assessment of physical and non-physical risks using Severity × Probability and produces a RAC.

DEA™ — DIMS Execution Algorithm is the DIMS decision mechanism used after risk assessment to determine the relative order of executable projects/tasks. When items share the same RAC, DEA uses objective system facts—such as verified dependencies, recorded deadlines, waiting age, readiness, and continuity—to discriminate among them. DEA is designed to minimize subjective human scoring.

Separation of purpose: DEA determines what should be executed first. Execution Performance Metrics measure how the work was actually executed.

Execution Performance Metrics — Separate from DEA

These metrics evaluate how work was actually executed. They do not determine RAC or execution priority.

Execution Performance Metrics are system-observed during/after execution. They are intentionally separated from pre-execution priority decisions.

APN — Abatement Priority Number

ACTUAL PHYSICAL HAZARDS ONLY. Use APN for physical occupational-safety, fire, or occupational-health hazards requiring abatement prioritization. Do not use APN for software, project, governance, deployment, administrative, or workflow risk.

CEI Inputs

Calculation

CEI = C ÷ (M × E)
APN = RAC (CEI)
Enter physical-hazard inputs and calculate.

Within identical RACs, lower CEI represents higher relative abatement priority. APN is not used to compare projects of different RACs.

Reference & Governance

RADBroader DIMS risk assessment service. Physical + non-physical governed risk.
DEADIMS Execution Algorithm. Determines relative execution order from objective system facts.
APNPhysical-hazard abatement prioritization only, using RAC and CEI.

RAD/RAC/APN concepts are maintained separately from DEA and the DIMS Execution Performance Metrics so risk assessment, execution ordering, execution measurement, and physical-hazard abatement remain distinct.
