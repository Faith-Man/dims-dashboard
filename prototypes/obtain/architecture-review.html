GOVERNING STRUCTURE — REVIEW BEFORE BUILD

Spartans1st™ Platform Hierarchy

This page inventories and separates the systems already established so the platform, Daily Development cycle, athlete development, risk governance, team operations and administration are not forced into one wheel.

Review status only. No existing splash screen, home circle, production DIMS/DOME page or main navigation has been replaced.
LAYER 1 — FOUNDATION & CONTENT ENGINE

Foundation + Dynamic LOGOS / RHEMA / DECLARATION

Foundation / Spartan StandardDynamic LOGOSDynamic RHEMADynamic DECLARATION

The daily content engine rotates Scripture, motivational/inspirational content and appropriate Spartan Standard material. LOGOS, RHEMA and DECLARATION are therefore not merely static pages; they also feed the athlete's changing daily experience.

LAYER 2 — DAILY DEVELOPMENT & EXECUTION

Continuous Development Cycle

LOGOS → RHEMA → FAITH → VISION → VISUALIZATION → DECLARATION → 🏗️DoiT! → RESULT → 3Es REVIEW → IMPROVE → LOGOS

The hierarchy and exact content of these ten stages remain under review. WIN™ is the center/outcome concept, not an additional numbered step.

LAYER 3 — ATHLETE DEVELOPMENT

Purpose, Preparation, Pursuit & Progress

MARKTRAINFIGHTPRESSPRIZEGoalsPersonal RecordsTraining AssignmentsRace / Performance RecordsMilestones

VISION establishes the destination; MARK identifies the specific goal/aim. TRAIN prepares. FIGHT contends. PRESS pursues the MARK. PRIZE identifies the objective/reward. Athlete Development records evidence of growth over time.

CROSS-PLATFORM GOVERNANCE LAYER

IAM™ → RAD → RAC → Mitigation / Action

IAM: IdentifyIAM: AssessIAM: MitigateRAD: Risk Assessment DomeRAC: Risk Assessment Code

IAM operates across training, readiness and athlete development. When an identified condition warrants structured assessment, RAD applies the approved Probability × Severity method. RAC communicates the resulting risk level. RAC categories, colors and thresholds are intentionally not invented here; they remain governed by the approved matrix.

LAYER 4 — TEAM & COACH OPERATIONS

Team Management

Team RosterAthlete ProfilesAttendanceTraining AssignmentsTraining CompletionMeets / EventsCoach AnnouncementsIAM Follow-UpTeam 3Es

Authorized coaches see the athlete-development information needed to observe readiness and well-being, review reported information and performance indicators, document follow-up and adjust training within their role and authority.

LAYER 5 — PROGRAM ADMINISTRATION

Resources & Stewardship

BudgetEquipment / NeedsResourcesForms / DocumentsSchedulingEvents

Administrative capabilities support the program without cluttering the athlete's daily execution experience.

ROLE-BASED EXPERIENCE

Athlete • Coach • Parent

Athlete — own experienceCoach — authorized development accessParent — linked-athlete parent-visible progress

Visibility is role-based and authorized. Parent-visible progress means visibility, not parent approval. The athlete experience remains focused while coach and administrative tools expose the additional functions required by those roles.
