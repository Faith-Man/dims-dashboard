RAD™ — Risk Assessment Dome is the DIMS cross-system risk-assessment service and working space for physical and non-physical governed risk.

DEA™ — DIMS Execution Algorithm determines relative execution order after risk assessment using objective recorded facts rather than subjective human scoring.

RAC applies to both physical and non-physical governed risk. APN does not. APN is reserved for actual physical occupational-safety, fire, or occupational-health hazards.

DEA™ — DIMS Execution Algorithm

When executable DIMS items share the same RAC, DEA uses objective recorded facts such as verified blocking dependencies, recorded deadlines, waiting age, readiness, and continuity to determine execution order.

EPI is retired as a separate priority layer. DEA owns execution ordering. Execution Performance Metrics measure execution after the work begins.

APN — Abatement Priority Number

ACTUAL PHYSICAL HAZARDS ONLY. Do not use APN for software, project, governance, deployment, administrative, or workflow risk.
