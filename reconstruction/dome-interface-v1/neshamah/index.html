NESHAMAH™

Hebrew:
נְשָׁמָה · neshamah · “breath”
Why This Name:
NESHAMAH was chosen because biblical breath imagery connects life, inspiration, and God-given understanding. The module emphasizes receiving and expressing Spirit-led understanding rather than merely accumulating information.
Job 32:8 (KJV):
“But there is a spirit in man: and the inspiration of the Almighty giveth them understanding.”
Function:
Presents current inspiration, understanding, Spirit-led direction, directives, and declarations while keeping creation and system machinery outside the everyday-user path.
