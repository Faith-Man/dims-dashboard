Vision

Build champions in life and sport.

Build a championship cross-country program that develops athletes of excellence, character, discipline, integrity, and purpose—prepared to succeed in competition and beyond it.

Mission

Develop disciplined runners of character, courage, and excellence.

Through purposeful training, accountability, preparation, teamwork, personal development, and competitive pursuit.

Program Goals

THE STANDARD

01 — Athlete Development

02 — Excellence

Pursue Excellence, Effectiveness, and Efficiency.

03 — Disciplined Training

Build consistent, purposeful preparation and responsible recovery.

04 — Character & Integrity

Cultivate courage, responsibility, sportsmanship, leadership, and integrity.

Reinforce the student-athlete responsibility to succeed academically.

Build unity, accountability, encouragement, communication, and shared purpose.

07 — Preparation & Confidence

Compete intelligently, confidently, and resiliently.

08 — Development Pipeline

Strengthen development from middle school through high school.

09 — Participation & Retention

Create an environment athletes want to join, grow in, contribute to, and remain part of.

10 — Long-Term Success

Pursue measurable improvement, personal records, team achievement, and a durable championship culture.
