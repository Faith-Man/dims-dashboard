LOGOS · The Written Word (Truth)

LOGOS

“So run, that ye may obtain.” — 1 Corinthians 9:24. LOGOS is the fixed, written foundation everything else in the WIN platform stands on: Scripture as truth, not opinion. Every RHEMA application, every IAM assessment, and every daily PRESS session is meant to trace back to a LOGOS reference.

See today's LOGOS reading on the Athlete Home →

RHEMA · The Applied Word (Direction)

RHEMA

Where LOGOS is the written word, RHEMA is that word applied to a specific moment — the coach's instruction before a meet, the verse an athlete leans on mid-race. “I press toward the mark for the prize…” — Philippians 3:14.

See how Coach delivers today's RHEMA direction →

IAM™ · Identify · Assess · Mitigate

IAM™ Check-In

IAM™ is the risk-and-readiness discipline behind the platform: identify a pattern (missed sessions, fatigue, attendance), assess it honestly, and mitigate it with a concrete action — before it becomes a bigger problem.

Open the Coach IAM™ action queue →

TRAIN · γυμνάζω (gymnazō) · Develop · Prepare

TRAIN

Gymnazō — to exercise, to discipline the body toward a purpose. TRAIN is the preparation layer: the assigned workouts, drills, and reps that make PRESS and FIGHT possible on meet day.

See today's training assignment →

FIGHT · ἀγωνίζομαι (agōnizomai) · Compete · Discipline · Endure

FIGHT

“I have fought a good fight, I have finished my course.” — 2 Timothy 4:7. FIGHT is the competitive discipline itself — showing up, competing with integrity, and enduring when it's hard.

Return to Athlete Home →

PRESS · διώκω (diōkō) · Pursue · Persevere

PRESS — The Spartan Standard

PRESS is pursuit without letting up — the standard the team is held to day over day, not just on meet day. It's today's tempo run, this week's mileage, the fourth consecutive training week completed.

See today's PRESS™ assignment →

PRIZE · βραβεῖον (brabeion) · The Prize · Focus

PRIZE™ Milestones

PRIZE keeps the goal in view — personal records, consistency milestones, and team recognition. Not every win is a race win; some are the fourth straight week of showing up.

See PRIZE™ milestones on Athlete Home →

The 3Es · Excellence · Effectiveness · Efficiency

The 3Es

The 3Es are the quality lens applied across every other module: doing it with excellence, doing the right thing (effectiveness), and doing it without waste (efficiency).

Foundation

Let Them Have Dominion

“And God said, Let us make man in our image, after our likeness: and let them have dominion…” — Genesis 1:26 (KJV). Spartans1st™ is built on the conviction that athletes are purposed, trained, and empowered to make a difference — on the field and beyond it. FIGHT. PRESS. WIN.™ — run with purpose.

Scriptural foundation for the WIN platform: 1 Corinthians 9:24–27, Philippians 3:14, 2 Timothy 4:7.
