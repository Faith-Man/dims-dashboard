OIKONOMOS™

Greek:
οἰκονόμος · oikonomos · “steward / household manager”
Why This Name:
OIKONOMOS was chosen because stewardship is the faithful administration of what another has entrusted. The module therefore focuses on responsibility, condition, obligations, resources, and accountable care rather than ownership for its own sake.
Luke 12:42 (KJV):
“And the Lord said, Who then is that faithful and wise steward, whom his lord shall make ruler over his household, to give them their portion of meat in due season?”
Function:
Shows the stewardship state of entrusted resources, obligations, assets, records, renewals, and material responsibilities; actionable work flows to TETELESTAI™ for execution.
Stewardship
Attention Now

Future live integration will surface due dates, renewals, exceptions, thresholds, and obligations here without forcing the everyday user into accounting or system-administration screens.

Resources

What has been entrusted: financial resources, property, vehicles, equipment, records, and other governed assets.

Obligations

What must be maintained, renewed, paid, documented, protected, or reviewed—without duplicating task records.

Stewardship History

Material changes, decisions, status, and stewardship history. Actionable work flows to TETELESTAI.

Boundary: OIKONOMOS is a stewardship module, not a replacement banking, payroll, tax, insurance, or generic accounting application. Commodity capabilities should be integrated when needed rather than rebuilt.
