## Source characteristics observed

The DMS baseline is a large conversational/system-development record rather than a clean software repository. The beginning of the document preserves early development of:

- Dominion1st / Divine ID assistant concepts;
- specialized AI-assistant identity separate from general ChatGPT usage;
- voice/personality and shortcut activation concepts;
- Framer-based interface/deployment discussion;
- task/reminder and priority-list concepts;
- early expectations that the assistant would connect to email, calendar, tasks, and operating tools.

This is important lineage evidence because DMS appears to have combined **assistant behavior, management, reminders/priorities, ministry tooling, interface design, and operating-system concepts** before later DIMS/DOME vocabulary was formalized.
